# Desportes 1593

Author: Maxim Egorov

Selected passages from a partial working reconstruction of the letter attributed to Baudouin Desportes, written to Pietro Aldobrandini in Paris on 22 July 1593. The investigation applies Satoshi Tomokiyo’s published polyphonic key and preserves the later comparison with Paolo Rosson’s reading.

- [Complete published article](article.md)
- [Methods](methods.md)
- [Readings and unresolved comparisons](readings.md)
- [Sources and prior work](sources.md)

The public [reading and replay package](../verification/readings/README.md) includes the [working French text](../verification/readings/evidence/Desportes_1593_evidence/Desportes_1593_evidence/letter_reading_FR.txt), [sign-alignment ledger](../verification/readings/evidence/Desportes_1593_evidence/Desportes_1593_evidence/sign_alignment.csv), and [transcription](../verification/readings/evidence/Desportes_1593_evidence/Desportes_1593_evidence/letter_transcription.json), with bounded internal checks. Source images are excluded; source glyphs and polyphonic letter choices remain interpretive. Later PAR evidence stays separate from this frozen working edition. This is not complete source verification. No new cipher key, complete solution or global priority is claimed.
