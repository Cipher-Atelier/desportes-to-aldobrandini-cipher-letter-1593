# New control observations, before the new target comparison

O01 contextual crop is read as `... commande deux choses my ont ...`. In deux, the first mark has an ascending oblique loop and a low rounded body. The middle e/u is low and connected; the right edge contains an oblique/crossed continuation compatible with x. Whole-word label DEUX is usable as an own contextual control, clearer than the earlier tentative d’eux label, but it is not externally certified and its joined e/u has no fully separate letter outlines.

In choses, the right-hand ending has connected small angular/curved ink after the preceding s. An e+terminal-s reading is contextually usable, but the ending does not provide two isolated outlines. The last small right-hand turn is not an independently detached sign.

Possible is readable in its own O04 context. Its final e has a rounded/low portion and a long rightward exit/flourish; the exit is part of a single terminal e reading, not automatically another letter. Nouvelle is readable in its own O05 context; its final e has a short upturned/rightward continuation. Both e-only controls therefore warn against equating any added-looking hook or exit with s.

These are observations of the named control windows, selected by the predefined word list. The full contextual control crops contain neighbouring ink; they are not certified exact character boundaries. They have no dependence on the intended quantity in V17. Roles, known readings and prior target observations remain known to the AI.
