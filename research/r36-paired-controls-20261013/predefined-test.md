# New paired controls for r36: criteria before primary comparison

AI work with known pages, key and candidate readings; not fully blind and not external expert verification. Previous PAR control on f176/f177, the first-fragment f188v/f185 test, L05 enumeration and all ordinary V17 controls are excluded.

Inventory candidates: f176r/v cipher versus contemporary f177r/v clear; f179r cipher versus contemporary f178r clear; f188r cipher versus contemporary f184r/v clear. Pair labels are attributed to Rosson’s pinned repository c7d3688324b0153663d0a3cce722fbe56e721730, not new discoveries. Its modern transcription was read with cipher beside it and expands abbreviations; it cannot be our independent label source.

Selected new primary test: first-line `force ung` in the f179r/f178r pair (Gallica f333/f331), excluding the preceding PAR sign. The modern file proposes this context, but we must independently read the actual open-copy ink before viewing the corresponding cipher. Preserve spelling and abbreviation uncertainty. If force/ung or their boundaries are ambiguous, no exact value is admitted. Do not add/delete letters or expand an unread abbreviation to fit cipher count; do not switch to another word to manufacture success.

Alignment must be supported by two legible neighbouring anchors/order, not page labels alone. Record raw cipher stroke groups/count and uncertainty before mapping the known key. One-to-one alphabetic values are allowed only if the independently read substring and definite code-unit boundaries agree without repairs; a special sign, cancellation, unclear boundary or extra unit disqualifies that exact positional label. A code class/allowed-letter fit alone is insufficient.

Detached E/R component: a positive control requires a labelled R or E with an actually detached oblique component plus main body, unambiguous grouping supported by the adjacent exact alignment and no competing neighbour assignment. It proves possible grouped execution, not automatic membership of r36’s short stroke. Absence in this one segment cannot prove such execution impossible.

H/B: require separately labelled U/V (H class) and O/B (B class), with distinguishable loop/stem topology and sufficient detail. Compare at native pixel scale, stating which of those traits survive in r36’s first mark. A broadly similar alphabet sign or same historical author does not establish same scribal hand. Hand compatibility must be assessed on several other forms; transfer remains conditional without attribution.

F/A: force’s F and ung’s N are candidate F/S and A/N controls. A decisive exclusion of A requires a comparably overinked A/N whose diagnostic tail/architecture remains independently visible, not only a clean long-stem A versus blotted F. Clean controls may supply limited shape comparison but cannot close dense-target topology by themselves.

Raw RGB source crops, original scale1; optional nearest-neighbour magnification only, no retouch/OCR/generation. Record source URLs, coordinates and hashes. Publish only own text and metadata, with images and full modern researchers’ texts staying local. Stop after the selected fragment. Give a checked-source inventory, admitted labels and specific unmet criteria; do not claim the entire archives or other fragments exhausted.
